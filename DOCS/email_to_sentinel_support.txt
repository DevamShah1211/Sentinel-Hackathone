OBSERVATION: CONCURRENT RTSP CONNECTIONS ARE ACCEPTED SERIALLY

Opening N simultaneous connections to 103.250.160.189:8554 from a single machine:

    Concurrent connections    Succeeded    Total time
    ----------------------    ---------    ----------
             2                  2 / 2         12 s
             4                  4 / 4         26 s
             6                  6 / 6         41 s
             8                  7 / 8         88 s

At eight connections, the per-connection accept times were 3.8, 7.8, 26.7, 33.3,
56.4, 60.0, 63.0 and 73.3 seconds - a queue rather than a load curve.

Throughout these tests our own machine remained at 4% CPU across 20 cores, with
no saturation of memory, decoder or network, which suggests the delay is on the
gateway side rather than the client's.

We also observed that HLS playlist requests
(https://cctv.corp8.cloud/<cam>/index.m3u8) currently take 6-17 seconds to
return. This is much improved from before your fix, but still slow enough that
browser HLS players time out during startup, so we now consume RTSP and
transcode locally for our operator view.
